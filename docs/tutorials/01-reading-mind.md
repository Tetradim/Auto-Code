# Tutorial 1: Reading the Bot's Mind in 60 Seconds

Don't watch candles. Watch 3 panels in the Mission Control dashboard.

**1. Market Weather (top right)**
If it's STORM ⛈️, the bot is OFF. This is why you're not trading. 80% of beginners blow up in storms.

**2. Edge Score (big middle gauge)**
This is its confidence 0-100.
- >70 GREEN: It sees a pattern it has won on before. Expect a trade.
- <30 RED: It's protecting you. Doing nothing IS the strategy.

**3. Current Mode**
SCANNING = waiting. IN LONG = bought. COOLDOWN = lost a trade, sitting out 30 mins.

**Rule:** A good bot sits still for 18 hours a day. A bad bot overtrades.
