# Tutorial 4: Understanding "The Brain" Dashboard

The Mission Control dashboard tells you **what** the bot is doing.  
This dashboard tells you **why**.

### How to read it in under 2 minutes:
1. Look at the big **Edge Score gauge**.  
   - Green (>70) = the bot sees a repeatable pattern.  
   - The table next to it shows the exact factors (Trend, Volume, Order Book, Funding Rate) and how much each contributed.

2. Check the three "senses" graphs:  
   - **Trend Compass** — is the wind blowing in one clear direction?  
   - **Volatility (ATR)** — is the sea calm or a hurricane?  
   - **Order Book Imbalance** — are buyers or sellers heavier right now?

3. Read the **Last Trade Narrative** text panel.  
   This is written in plain English so you can learn the bot's logic without knowing technical indicators.

**Key mindset:** A great trading bot spends most of its time doing nothing. When The Brain is mostly gray/yellow, it is protecting your capital. That is winning.
