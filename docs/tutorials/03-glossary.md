# Glossary for Non-Traders

- **PnL:** Profit and Loss. Your money score.
- **Long/Short:** Long = we make money if price goes UP. Short = we make money if price goes DOWN.
- **Edge:** Your advantage over random. 52% win rate with 1:2 risk/reward = you get rich slow.
- **Drawdown:** From your peak money, how far you fell. A 10% drawdown is normal. 30% is not.
- **Leverage:** Sentinel defaults to 1x. DO NOT CHANGE THIS until you've watched 100 trades.
- **Stop Loss:** Automatic "I was wrong" button. This is what keeps you alive.
