# Tutorial 2: Why Did It Just Trade?

Open the "Why Did We Enter?" table.

You'll see: `reason="TrendFlip+VolumeSpike" edge="82" side="LONG"`

Translation for humans:
1. **TrendFlip:** The market just turned up
2. **VolumeSpike:** Big players are buying with it
3. **Edge 82:** In backtests, this exact setup won 68% of the time
4. **Stop:** -0.8%

The bot is NOT guessing. It's playing a statistical hand.
